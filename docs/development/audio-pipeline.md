# Capture, VAD and recognition lifecycle

The detailed current TDT policy and historical measurements are in [live TDT chunking](../live-tdt-vad-chunking.md); consult `SpeechEngine`, `TdtChunkPolicy`, `VadTrailingTrim`, `HistoryVadTransform` and `RecordingLimits` for current behavior. Original bounded 16 kHz mono PCM is authoritative, retained independently of every decoder input and saved for successful History. Never silently drop samples, mutate saved PCM, auto-submit a transcript or infer recognition accuracy from complete range coverage. Capture queues must hold the full capture cap so slow inference cannot block the microphone or skip a chunk. Retain ownership until in-flight recorder reads and native operations have drained; an active JNI call may not be interruptible.

## TDT live microphone

When chunking is enabled, separate VAD and a single serialized offline TDT worker use ordered, non-overlapping descriptors into original PCM. A detected pause after five seconds of cumulative confirmed speech OR approximately three seconds of VAD-negative audio following possible speech closes a range; reset the speech count at a boundary. Neither is a maximum duration. On Stop decode a tail of at least one second; a shorter tail may be skipped only with available VAD and no possible speech, even if it is the entire recording. Preserve unconfirmed candidates; on VAD failure decode the unsplit remainder. Drain ranges in order, snapshot final-text settings at Stop, assemble once and polish once. On chunk/range failure drain and retry the entire original capture before publishing anything. Cancellation suppresses all queued/native output and final effects.

Silero's `maxSpeechDuration` in sherpa-onnx 1.13.8 is adaptive rather than a timer: after the threshold it raises the speech threshold and shortens minimum silence, but still needs classified non-speech. Use its probability API or promptly pop segments rather than letting an active utterance accumulate indefinitely. Visual VAD is advisory, independent of the chunking toggle; disabling chunking decodes the full recording on Stop but can retain the visual indicator. VAD failure leaves recording active and visibly unavailable. Speech-activity background and local chunk-decoding stop-square tint are independent observers, guarded by session and capture state.

TDT VAD chunks trim classified negative tail after the last complete 32 ms positive frame; if none is positive, preserve that chunk's original trailing input. Preserve an unclassified partial final frame even if removing preceding frames creates a discontinuity. Optional internal silence removal is default-off, classifies runs over the complete recording, protects run/chunk edges and can still cut quiet speech. Never claim these transformations are lossless recognition: VAD can remove speech or word endings; silence can hallucinate.

## Streaming and stop

The current selected streaming model is multilingual Nemotron 3.5. Keep one online stream for a whole live session or saved file; no VAD construction or gating, including History/import. Feed all PCM, pauses included, in bounded blocks on a separate worker; snapshot the active model per session. Disable/dim TDT-only VAD controls without modifying their stored values. Hypotheses remain internal; PARTIAL IPC is reserved. At Stop snapshot final text settings, drain backlog, feed finalization silence separately from saved PCM, signal `inputFinished`, drain ready frames and return one final result without a second full-file decode. InputFinished alone cannot necessarily flush incomplete NeMo encoder chunks. Cancel skips finalization and result but waits for native ownership to be safely released. A slow device can make Stop wait for backlog; no latency or quality guarantee follows from host tests.

At the live capture cap, stop/release the microphone, retain PCM and wait for explicit Stop/Cancel, with a cue; do not finalize or submit automatically. Trace the IPC deadline and client timeout when changing the cap. A notification shade or transient focus loss does not cancel capture, while screen-off/lock and local service teardown follow the local safety policy; they must not hijack external ownership.

## VAD equivalence checks

For incremental removal, retain original sample coordinates and PCM. Delay an open negative-run boundary until the run ends or it is at least the end guard behind the observed end; merely reaching the eligibility minimum is not enough. Keep incomplete final frames unclassified and report discontinuities from retaining a partial frame after removing classified predecessors. Negative VAD is not proof of silence.

Use deterministic synthetic input against the actual compiled batch and streaming transforms: randomized probabilities, frame-aligned chunk boundaries and partial-frame lengths, byte-for-byte output comparisons for matching original ranges, and an independent per-sample oracle so shared bugs cannot pass. Include cross-boundary negative runs, just-below/above/equal thresholds, exact frame endings, bounded lookahead, unchanged PCM, capture-cap input and draining on explicit end. Separately exercise cancellation/fault recovery in the actual capture/decoder pipeline; transform probes do not verify device behavior. A Java host probe against Gradle Kotlin output and cached Kotlin stdlib is an option if no `kotlinc` executable exists; keep scratch probes outside the repo.
